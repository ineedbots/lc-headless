## Feature follow-up (2026-11-05)

- Multilocs: `Loc.id` remains the scene/packet identity; `Loc.type_id` is the visible child. Names and actions come from that child. A hidden child disappears from queries, and a retained snapshot becomes invalid when its child changes. Player and NPC snapshots also expose `animation_id` and `animation_tick`, while `animation` retains its existing expiry behavior.
- JiveRC: select `Cosmic runes`, start in Zanaris or the Cosmic altar, and carry the matching talisman for inside meetings. The Dramen staff survives litter cleanup and bank deposits. `bots/nature_crafter.py` and its saved settings remain supported; `bots/jive_rc.py` exposes the upstream name.
- Fisher: enable `three_tick_fly` with the fly fishing method. Cooking and mule modes must be Off. Catches are dropped within the action batch even when banking is enabled; bank trips replenish the rod, feathers, knife and carrier log. When the bank cannot provide the kit, it takes the knife/axe ground spawns and chops a carrier log. This path also works with `bank=false`; other unshipped tick profiles remain Off.
- MuleDepot: every participant chooses the same bank. Dump mules send the exact configured item list to a supermule; supply sends each configured client its loadout across as many batches as necessary. Item names and ids resolve and merge before planning. Transfers require confirmed closure plus an inventory change. Three empty stock reads stop dumping or mark supply shortages, which are reported per client. Delivery accounting lasts for the current script run, as upstream does; restarting Supply begins a fresh delivery run.

### MuleCrafter settings migration

The new default `meeting_point` is `Altar (inside)`. Each mule must start with its matching talisman; choose `Ruins (outside)` to keep the old meeting location. The crafter stays at the altar between deliveries and exits for a due bank visit. Active trades take priority over bank and movement tasks.

`bank_fill` now means **allow crafter bank visits**, including startup cleanup. When false, the crafter must start with its talisman and cannot run without partners. When true, `trades_per_bank` controls banking: 0 selects solo crafting and banks on empty; a positive value enables mule trades and banks after that many confirmed deliveries. Existing paired configurations should set a positive trade count. The existing `bank_fill=true` default is retained locally.
